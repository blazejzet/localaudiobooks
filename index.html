A look inside

Podcasts and radio drama

Follow shows from recommendations, your country’s top charts or a search of the podcast directory — or paste any feed address. Episodes stream straight away, newest first, each with its own position; download only what you want offline.

Your folder is your library

Point the app at a folder of audiobooks on your device or in iCloud Drive — one subfolder of MP3 or AAC files per book. Manage everything in Files or Finder; share iCloud folders with family.

Made for every screen

A Liquid Glass design for iOS 26. On iPhone the sources sit in a bar of icons at the bottom that tucks away as you scroll; on iPad and the unfolded iPhone Duo they move to a rail on the right edge, right by your thumb.

Built for long listens

  • Resume exactly where you stopped
  • Sleep timer — minutes or end of chapter
  • Speed 0.8–2.5×, remembered per book
  • Skip intervals you choose, chapters, bookmarks
  • Lock screen, headphones and CarPlay
  • Background playback

Private by design

The app collects no data. The network is used only for catalogues, podcasts, downloads and cover art.
