A look inside

Bring your own feeds

Paste the address of any podcast or RSS feed — or of a web page that links to one, like a LibriVox book page. Each feed becomes an audiobook and its episodes the chapters, in listening order.

Your folder is your library

Point the app at a folder of audiobooks on your device or in iCloud Drive — one subfolder of MP3 or AAC files per book. Manage everything in Files or Finder; share iCloud folders with family.

Made for every screen

On iPad your library becomes a grid of covers. On iPhone Duo the app fits the compact outer screen as a one-hand player and switches to the cover grid on the wide inner screen.

Built for long listens

  • Resume exactly where you stopped
  • 30-second skips and variable speed
  • Chapter list and whole-book progress
  • Bookmarks, favourites and finished flags
  • Lock screen, headphones and CarPlay
  • Background playback

Private by design

The app collects no data. The network is used only to browse catalogues, download titles and fetch cover art.
