Support

Adding a free catalogue

Open Libraries → + → Add remote source, pick a catalogue (yours are listed first), then tap Browse titles.

Adding a podcast or RSS feed

Add the Your feeds source, open it and tap +. Paste a feed address or a web page that links to one (for example a LibriVox book page).

Adding your own files

Create a folder with one subfolder per book containing its MP3 or AAC files (optional: cover.jpg, info.txt). Then + → Add folder library and pick the parent folder.

A download failed

Open the title and tap Download again — files already on your device are kept and only the missing ones are fetched.

Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub.
