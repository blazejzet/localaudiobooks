Support

Turning on a free catalogue

Open Settings (the gear at the top) and switch the catalogue on — it gets its own tab in the tab bar (on iPhone, past five tabs, under More). Open a title and tap Listen now or Download.

Following a podcast

Open the Podcasts tab and tap the magnifier at the top: type the show’s name and tap +, or paste the address of its feed or web page and choose Follow this address.

Searching

The magnifier at the top searches the tab you are on — everything from Listen. Results are split into what is on your device and what you can still download.

Adding your own files

Create a folder with one subfolder per book containing its MP3 or AAC files (optional: cover.jpg, info.txt). Then Settings → Add folder and pick the parent folder.

A download failed

Open the title and tap Download again — files already on your device are kept and only the missing ones are fetched.

Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub.
